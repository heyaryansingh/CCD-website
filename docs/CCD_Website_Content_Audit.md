## CRITICAL ANALYSIS: CCD Website (Outsider Perspective)

### ✅ WHAT WORKS / IS IMPRESSIVE
- **Mission clarity**: The H.E.A.R.T. and C.L.E.A.R. acronym frameworks are memorable and communicate impact well.
- **Membership structure**: Having tiered pricing for individuals, residents, and businesses with clear perks is a real strength — very few community orgs do this.
- **Range of programs**: Clean & Green, Co-op Market, Brick Campaign, Tool Bank, community farming — the breadth is genuinely impressive for a community org.
- **Brick campaign page**: The fundraisingbrick.com integration is creative and gives donors a tangible, permanent way to contribute.
- **Before/after photos**: The Clean & Green work page effectively demonstrates real impact.
- **Contact info**: Address, phone, and email are prominently listed in the footer on every page.
- **Wix Multilingual**: Language selector (EN) suggests accessibility awareness.

### ❌ WHAT DOESN'T WORK / LOOKS BAD / IS MISSING

#### Design & Visual
- **Logo placement**: Logo sits in the dead center of the header above the nav. This is a Wix default pattern — looks amateurish, wastes vertical space, pushes content down. Professional sites put the logo in the top-left of the nav bar.
- **Header is huge and dead space**: 250px+ of white space just for the logo before the nav bar. On every single page. Users have to scroll just to see content.
- **Navigation bar color**: The gold/yellow nav bar with all-caps text in a generic serif font feels dated (early 2010s aesthetic). It's reminiscent of a Word document.
- **Hero slideshow**: Blurry, dark stock-photo-adjacent backgrounds with white text. The slides auto-rotate slowly. No CTAs (no buttons). Just text floating on blurred images. Very basic Wix template behavior.
- **Typography**: A mix of serif (Georgia/Times) and sans-serif throughout the site with no consistent typographic hierarchy. Headings feel random. Multiple different font sizes and weights used inconsistently.
- **No hero image of actual CCD work**: The slideshow backgrounds are extremely blurry. First impression is murky. ReBuild Metro uses a crisp aerial of real Baltimore streets — CCD should do the same with actual Irvington/Frederick Ave photos.
- **Color palette is undefined**: Yellow nav, white backgrounds, random black text, no brand consistency across pages. The Clean & Green page uses bold black, which clashes with everything else.
- **The "Programs & Workforce Development" page** is embarrassingly thin — just two partner logos (The 4th Brew and DeWalt) with brief descriptions. No programs are actually listed. This is a critical gap.

#### Content & Structure
- **Events page is empty**: "No events at the moment" is the entire page. No archive, no calendar, no upcoming announcements. Looks abandoned.
- **Photos page**: Presumably a gallery, but it's a Wix gallery widget with no additional context, labels, or captions.
- **"What is a Co-op?" page duplicates About content**: The entire H.E.A.R.T. section and "Our Story" section are copy-pasted from the About page. Completely redundant. The page answers "What is a cooperative?" with a one-sentence definition then pivots to CCD boilerplate.
- **No Tool Bank page**: Tracy's email specifically mentions a Tool Bank, but there is no dedicated page or section for it. It's referenced only as a membership benefit in tiny bullet points.
- **No Center for Social Impact dedicated page**: There's a section heading "Center for Social Impact" on the Expansion Goals page with no body text — just images. This is a major initiative with no real web presence.
- **No donation impact metrics**: The donate buttons go straight to PayPal with no context — no dollar amounts tied to outcomes, no fundraising thermometer, no stories.
- **No staff/team page**: Who is CCD? No bios, no photos of leadership. The website feels anonymous.
- **No "Join Us" / Volunteer CTA**: Beyond membership, there's no way to volunteer, partner, or otherwise get involved.
- **Footer copyright says 2025**: It's 2026. Small but unprofessional.
- **"Our Work" email** uses `francis@ccdgroup.org` while the main site uses `info@ccdgroup.org` — inconsistent.

#### UX / Navigation
