What was once abandoned
can become a beacon.

In 2023, CCD acquired the former Jarbo Brothers Storage facility — a 5,000-square-foot building that sat empty while the neighborhood was told to wait. We stopped waiting. Backed by BRED, the Neighborhood Design Center, and ReGEN Consulting, we're turning decades of disinvestment into Baltimore's most compelling cooperative infrastructure project.
