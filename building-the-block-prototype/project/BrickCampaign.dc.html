THE STORY

One building.
A thousand names.

For decades, 3932 Frederick Ave stored boxes. Soon it will hold a grocery store, retail stalls, a multimedia center — the economic engine of a neighborhood that decided to own its own future. The Brick Campaign lets anyone lay a piece of that foundation: your name, your family's, or the name of someone you want remembered on this block, engraved in the building itself.
