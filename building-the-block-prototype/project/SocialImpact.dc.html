THE VISION

5,000 square feet
of possibility.

The former Jarbo Brothers Storage facility at 3932–3934 Frederick Avenue is becoming the Center for Social Impact: a cooperative grocery store, a multimedia center, retail stalls for local makers, and flexible space for training and gathering — all owned by the community it serves.

CCD acquired the building in 2023 with our partners at BRED, the Baltimore Roundtable for Economic Democracy. The Neighborhood Design Center and ReGEN Consulting Group are shaping its design and development.
