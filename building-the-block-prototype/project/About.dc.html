OUR STORY

We are Baltimore's cooperative future.

SINCE 2020

A co-op grown
from the block up.

Cooperative Community Development Inc. was founded in January 2020 by neighbors who believed disinvested communities don't need saving from outside — they need ownership from within. What began in 2019 as a single reclaimed lot, the Oasis @ 240, grew into a member-owned cooperative operating across housing, food access, green infrastructure, and local enterprise.

In 2023, CCD acquired the former Jarbo Brothers Storage facility at 3932 Frederick Avenue — a 5,000 sq ft building that will become the Center for Social Impact: Baltimore's most ambitious cooperative economic hub. With backing from the Baltimore Roundtable for Economic Democracy, the Neighborhood Design Center, and ReGEN Consulting, every step is member-governed: one member, one vote.

Our model is not just for Irvington. It is a replicable blueprint for any community that has been told to wait for investment that never comes — and decided to build it themselves.

"Creating a local and global path to self-sustainable communities."

WHAT IS A CO-OP?

A co-op is yours.

A cooperative is an autonomous association of people united to meet common economic, social and cultural needs through a jointly-owned, democratically-controlled enterprise. At CCD, that means Irvington residents own the market, the tools, and the future being built on Frederick Avenue — and the model we're proving here can travel to any neighborhood that chooses self-determination over dependency.

01 Members join Anyone can become a member — residents, neighbors, and local businesses alike.
02 Members govern One member, one vote. Every voting member has an equal say in what CCD does next.
03 Benefits are shared Surplus flows back to members and the neighborhood — not to distant shareholders.
