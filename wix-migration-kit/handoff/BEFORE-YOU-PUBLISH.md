# Before You Publish — Quick Check

A few quick checks before you hit Publish. Takes about 10 minutes. Full detail lives in `ACCESSIBILITY.md` if you want to dig deeper.

- [ ] **Every picture has a description.** Click each image and check there's a description written in the "Alt Text" field — not left blank, not just the file name.
- [ ] **Each page has one main headline.** Look at the page — there should be exactly one big title at the top that says what the page is about, not two or three competing ones.
- [ ] **Link text says where it goes.** Read your links out loud without looking at the page around them. "Learn more" alone is confusing if there are several — say "Learn more about the co-op market" instead.
- [ ] **Text is dark-on-light or light-on-dark — never gold on white.** Gold text on a white or light background is hard to read for many people; gold only works as a button background with dark text on top, or as text on a dark green or black background.
- [ ] **Every video has captions.** Play the video with the sound off and check the words appear on screen and are correct — not the raw auto-generated captions.
- [ ] **You can reach everything with just the Tab key.** Click once on the page, then press Tab repeatedly — make sure you can get to every link, button, and form field, and that you can always see where you are.
- [ ] **Nothing was typed by hand into a color or font box.** Use the app's saved styles (the `.ccd-` options) instead of picking a custom color or font — this keeps colors readable and consistent.

**Made a mistake?** If you changed a **page** — text, a picture, the layout — go to Site → History → Restore. If you changed **content** (a team member, news post, event or project), Site History will **not** bring it back; see "Undoing a mistake" in `EDITOR-HANDBOOK.md` first. For the reasoning behind any check above, see `ACCESSIBILITY.md`.
